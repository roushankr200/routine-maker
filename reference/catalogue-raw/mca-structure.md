# MCA — course structure extracted 2026-11-11
Source PDF (OFFICIAL): https://nita.ac.in/Syllabus_MCA_CSE_CSE10090.pdf (42 pp, "Proposed Course Structure & Syllabus for MCA (Six Semester Course)", effective batches admitted from 2016–17; structure tables pp. 3–5). Full subject codes ARE printed (MCA01C01 style). Programme total: 140 credits.
IMPORTANT for the routine model: MCA labs are 0-0-6 (6 practical hrs/week, 3 cr) — in the block model that is weeklyHours 6 with sessionHours 3 = two 3h lab sessions per week. Theory weeklyHours = L+T.
Cross-check vs routine sheet: Sem 1 DFS/DCLD/DMS/PMP/WT ✓ (sheet "DFS" = Data and File Structure; its lab is titled Data Structure Laboratory) · Sem 3 CETW/DMDW/DAA/NM/SE ✓ · Sem 5 AI/CD ✓, electives E1/E2 from {DIP, E-COMM, Intro Crypto, Mobile Computing, Multimedia, WCN} — sheet takes WCN (E1) + DIP (E2) ✓.
